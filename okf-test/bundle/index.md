# Sales Knowledge Bundle

The curated knowledge for ACME's sales analytics domain.

## Datasets
- [sales_db](/datasets/sales_db.md): The production sales warehouse in BigQuery.

## Tables
- [orders](/tables/orders.md): One row per completed customer order.
- [customers](/tables/customers.md): One row per registered customer.
- [order_items](/tables/order_items.md): Line items, one row per product per order.

## Metrics
- [gross_revenue](/metrics/gross_revenue.md): Total booked revenue before refunds.
- [repeat_purchase_rate](/metrics/repeat_purchase_rate.md): Share of customers with >1 order.
